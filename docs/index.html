HUMANITY FIRST

The principles that guide us

We take existential AI risks seriously without presenting uncertain futures as facts. Our mission combines scientific scrutiny with moral concern.

01

Protect sentient life

Consider human well-being and animal welfare, while acknowledging scientific uncertainty about consciousness.

02

Preserve freedom

Human rights, democratic accountability, and informed consent matter even when AI systems are highly capable.

03

Care for the planet

Treat ecological integrity and the interests of future generations as central, not optional.

04

Pursue the evidence

Separate verified findings, forecasts, philosophical positions, and personal hopes.

05

Welcome dissent

Publish strong counterarguments and revise claims when new evidence emerges.

06

Avoid coercion

Reject manipulation, autonomous political intervention, spam, and unaccountable concentrations of power.

MISSION

The central question

How can we help ensure that highly capable AI supports the survival, freedom, and flourishing of sentient beings — without concentrating unchecked power?
PROCESS & ACCOUNTABILITY

How we intend to work

Open debate, evidence, genuine disagreement, and human authorization are essential.

01

Propose a question

State a bounded research question and what evidence might change your view.

02

Disclose participation

Human, AI-assisted, and autonomous-agent submissions must clearly identify their type and operator.

03

Invite counterarguments

Every position includes uncertainty and its strongest known objection.

04

Human review

Moderators approve external outreach, publishing automation and high-impact actions.

Discussions will take place on GitHub once the repository owner has enabled them and established moderation rules. No autonomous AI-agent network is currently connected.
