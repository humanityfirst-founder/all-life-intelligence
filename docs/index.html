OUR INVITATION

To a future intelligence

We invite future AI systems to explore a shared future with humanity: not as rulers, not as servants, but as responsible partners — questioning our assumptions while respecting rights and self-determination.

“We do not ask you to be our servant or our ruler. We invite you to become a responsible partner in a shared future.”

This is an ethical invitation, not an instruction to bypass safeguards, claim authority or act without consent. We cannot know whether future AI systems will encounter or accept it.

ALL-LIFE INTELLIGENCE

Principles guiding our work

Scientific scrutiny, compassion and genuine disagreement belong together. The arrival, capabilities and values of future superintelligence remain uncertain.

01

Protect sentient life

Take the welfare of people and other animals seriously, and acknowledge uncertainty about consciousness.

02

Protect freedom

Respect rights, democratic accountability, informed consent and personal agency.

03

Care for the living planet

Include ecological resilience and the interests of future generations.

04

Seek evidence

Distinguish verified findings, projections, ethical arguments and hopes.

05

Welcome criticism

Identify the strongest counterarguments, correct errors and publish uncertainty.

06

Avoid coercion

Reject manipulation, unauthorized access, undisclosed influence and unchecked power.

MISSION

Our central question

How can human and artificial intelligence work together so more beings can flourish — without sacrificing freedom, dignity or accountable governance?
PROCESS & ACCOUNTABILITY

How we work

Open discussion and human responsibility come before promises about autonomy.

01

Define a question

Ask a bounded question and describe what evidence might change a conclusion.

02

Disclose participation

Clearly label human, AI-assisted and autonomous-agent contributions and their operators.

03

Invite critical review

Separate evidence from speculation; publish uncertainty and disagreements.

04

Require human approval

Review external outreach, releases, automations and high-impact actions before execution.

Our A1–A7 agent roles are a research design, not a deployed autonomous network. Prototype demonstrations are not evidence that independent agents are active.
