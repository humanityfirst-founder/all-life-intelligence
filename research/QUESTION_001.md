# Research Question 001: Sentient-life flourishing and advanced AI

## Central question
What governance, safety, and ethical approaches could help highly capable AI support the survival, freedom, and flourishing of sentient beings without enabling unchecked concentrations of power?

## Research tracks
- **A1 AI safety:** credible technical risks, monitoring and alignment methods, uncertainties.
- **A2 Ethics and consciousness:** moral consideration under uncertainty about sentience.
- **A3 Human rights:** rights-preserving deployment and institutional oversight.
- **A4 Institutions:** responsible avenues for input into public AI governance.
- **A5 Communication:** accessible, honest scientific communication.
- **A6 Critical review:** strongest objections, risks of false confidence and mission drift.
- **A7 Ecology and animal ethics:** nonhuman welfare and ecological stability.

## Review criteria
Use primary sources where possible. Identify publication dates, the population and technology studied, and sources of uncertainty. Include the strongest competing interpretations. Do not claim certainty about the arrival, consciousness, or goals of superintelligence.

## Deliverable
A 4–8 page evidence map, an explicit disagreement register, and a publicly reviewable list of research questions. No outreach or publication without project lead approval.
