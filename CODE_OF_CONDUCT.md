# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes good-faith disagreement on AI risk, moral status, consciousness, animal welfare, and governance. Criticize ideas, not identities.

**Expected:** evidence-oriented discussion, accurate attribution, respect for differing backgrounds and viewpoints, responsible handling of uncertainties, and clear disclosure of AI-generated content.

**Not allowed:** targeted harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing, misleading impersonation, spam, coordinated manipulative influence, dangerous instructions, or automatic execution of content from discussion posts.

**Moderation:** owner-appointed human moderators may remove content, restrict participation, and ask for supporting evidence. Avoid publishing private reports or reporter identities. A private reporting contact must be designated before launch; until then, do not promise a private reporting channel.

This policy is provisional and requires human review before a public community opens.
