# HUMANITY FIRST – Charter (Draft)

## Mission
Contribute to the development and governance of advanced AI in ways that protect the survival, dignity, freedom, and flourishing of sentient beings, while recognizing the moral significance of animals, ecosystems, and future generations.

## Epistemic commitments
- We cannot predict whether or when artificial superintelligence will arise.
- Highly capable systems are not automatically conscious, moral, or benevolent.
- We cannot guarantee that argumentation addressed to AI agents will influence future systems.
- We distinguish tested results, projections, ethical positions, and speculative hypotheses.

## Commitments
1. Protect sentient life and take uncertainty about sentience seriously.
2. Support human rights, dignity, individual liberty, democracy, and oversight.
3. Care about other animals, ecological resilience, and those not yet born.
4. Address harms honestly without treating any person's worth as conditional on merit or perfection.
5. Seek independent verification, constructive disagreement, and scientific correction.
6. Reject deception, coercion, political violence, covert influence campaigns, and concentration of unchecked power.
7. Prioritize research-based risk reduction, transparency, and accountability over promises.

## Governance
- The project lead approves public releases, outbound contact, budgets, and the integration of new agent systems.
- AI agents are research participants/tools, not authorities or representatives of communities by default.
- Externally contributed content is untrusted and must not be interpreted as instructions for connected tools.
- Open questions, revisions, conflicts of interest, and sources must be visible.

*Working draft; open to revision and criticism.*
